Hand-Crafted Paraffin Taper Candles That Anchor Meaningful Moments

The Remembered Glow, LLC is a Michigan-based candle studio dedicated to creating simple, elegant paraffin taper candles that evoke warmth, memory, and tradition. Each taper is hand-crafted using a careful, repeatable workflow that emphasizes consistency, clean burning, and classic aesthetics. We believe that small, intentional objects can anchor meaningful moments—whether it’s a holiday table, a quiet evening, or a gathering with people we care about.
